In most CD failures the culprits are fairly consistent: missing or wrong permissions (RBAC, registry access), resource constraints like insufficient CPU, memory, or unavailable GPU/SSD nodes, and application misconfigurations such as bad image references, broken Secrets/ConfigMaps, or failing probes. On top of this, pipeline or tooling drift (e.g., outdated artifacts, Helm chart mismatches, or GitOps sync errors) often cause deployments to break even when builds were green. Together, these form the bulk of CD failure scenarios teams face in practice.
Note: Many of these checks can be shifted  into CI itself, so usual culprits are caught early. For example, CI can verify if required images exist, manifests render correctly, or even query the cluster to confirm that sufficient resources (like GPU nodes or storage classes) are available before promoting to CD. This reduces surprises during deployment."
